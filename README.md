# Criminal Rush Game

Beneath the shining skyline of Nocturne lies darkness. A ruthless criminal syndicate is expanding its influence through fear, corruption, and attacks on innocent civilians. With every crime committed, their network grows stronger, bringing Nocturne one step closer to falling under their control. Standing against them is an unlikely force: a team of rookie detectives. Civilians by day and investigators by night, they must gather evidence, expose criminal operations, and bring the syndicate to justice before it's too late. Every decision shapes the city's future. 
Will you save Nocturne City… or seize control of it? 

This is a digital version of the Criminal Rush card game.
